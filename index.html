Secure, read-only verification

You'll be taken to TrueLayer, our secure banking partner, to sign in with your bank and approve access.

We'll only see your bank name, account name, the last 4 digits of your account number, account type and the name of the account holder. We can't see your balance or transactions, and we can't make payments.

Your bank login details are never shared with us. You can cancel at any time.
