Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

James Studio Verify ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our identity verification service.

This Privacy Policy is intended to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Data Controller Information

Data Controller: James Studio (trading as James Studio Verify)

Contact: hello@jamesstudio.uk

Address: Manchester, United Kingdom

3. Information We Collect

We collect the following information through our identity verification process:

  • Bank Account Information: When you choose to verify your identity using your bank account, we collect limited account details including account holder name, bank name, account type, and the last 4 digits of your account number.
  • Technical Data: We may collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and usage logs for security and debugging purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases for processing your personal data:

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): You provide explicit consent when you choose to connect your bank account through TrueLayer for identity verification purposes.
  • Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f)): We have a legitimate interest in verifying your identity to prevent fraud and ensure the security of our services.
  • Contractual Necessity (Article 6(1)(b)): Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you where identity verification is a condition of service.

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at hello@jamesstudio.uk.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the purpose of identity verification:

  • To verify that you are the owner of the bank account you provide
  • To prevent fraud and ensure the security of our services
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements including anti-money laundering (AML) and know your customer (KYC) obligations
  • To improve our services and security measures

4. Third-Party Services: TrueLayer

We use TrueLayer, a regulated Open Banking provider, to securely connect to your bank account. When you use our bank verification service:

  • You will be redirected to TrueLayer's secure authentication page
  • TrueLayer handles the direct connection to your bank using Open Banking APIs
  • Your bank login credentials are never shared with us or stored on our servers
  • TrueLayer only shares the specific account information we request with your consent

TrueLayer is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) as an Account Information Service Provider (AISP). You can learn more about TrueLayer's privacy practices at truelayer.com/privacy.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Access controls and authentication for our systems

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Bank Verification Data: Retained for 12 months from the date of verification, unless required to be retained longer for legal or regulatory purposes.
  • Technical Logs: Retained for up to 6 months for security and debugging purposes.

After the retention period expires, we securely delete or anonymise your data in accordance with Article 5(1)(e) of the UK GDPR (storage limitation).

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • TrueLayer: As a regulated Open Banking provider authorised by the FCA, TrueLayer processes your bank data to provide the verification service. TrueLayer is our data processor under a written agreement that includes data protection obligations compliant with Article 28 of the UK GDPR.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred to the new owner.

8. International Data Transfers

Your personal information is processed within the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area (EEA). We do not transfer your personal information outside the UK/EEA unless:

  • The transfer is to a country that the UK government has recognised as having adequate data protection laws
  • We have appropriate safeguards in place (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect your information
  • You have given your explicit consent to the transfer

9. Your Rights

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right to be informed (Article 13 & 14): You have the right to be informed about how we use your personal data.
  • Right of access (Article 15): You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16): You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure (Article 17): You can request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing (Article 18): You can request that we restrict the processing of your personal information.
  • Right to data portability (Article 20): You can request that we transfer your data to another organisation.
  • Right to object (Article 21): You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Article 7(3)): You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@jamesstudio.uk. We will respond to your request within one month in accordance with Article 12(3) of the UK GDPR.

10. Cookies and Tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and for security purposes. Our Cookie Policy provides detailed information about the cookies we use and your choices regarding them.

11. Complaints
